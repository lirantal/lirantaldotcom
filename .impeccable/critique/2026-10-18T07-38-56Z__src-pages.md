## Overall Impression

The editorial system from PR #12 is well made; the opportunity is extending it to the speaking hub, blog and shared chrome, which still speak the template's louder dialect and hold nearly every measured defect.

## What's Working

- Evidence over claims: computed stats, preprint labels, npm caveats. Exactly what the brief asks for and what a technical audience trusts.
- `portfolio.css`: underlined links at 45% color, visible focus outlines, numbered uppercase H2s; the soft-black dark mode reads well, including inline code.
- Speaking data UX: linkable filters, `aria-live` counts, a recovering empty state, and a hero that follows the DESIGN.md grouping.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Speaking hub and blog index break the "no decorative gradients" brief.** Gradient headlines (`speaking/index.astro:51`, `PageIntro.astro:14`, `[...blog]/[...page].astro:40`), tilted gradient frame (`speaking/index.astro:69`), gradient year bars (`:197`), gradient CTA (`InviteCTA.astro:9`), pink book promo. *Fix:* solid text with a blue accent phrase, plain rounded photo, solid bars, quiet `.surface`-style CTA, blue promo. *Command:* `$impeccable quieter`, then `$impeccable polish`.
- **[P1] Organizer conversion path is mislabelled and ends at a LinkedIn profile.** "Invite me to speak →" on the homepage goes to `/speaking`; the same label there goes to LinkedIn with no external cue; no email, form or logistics. *Fix:* honest labels and ↗ cues now; a preferred contact channel and "how to invite me" block need the owner's decision. *Command:* `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Blog post template is legacy and hard to read.** ~105-115 characters per line (`SinglePost.astro:43` `max-w-5xl`), unnamed share icons, a pink promo with the cover clipped to "IRAN TAL" by `rounded-full`, long inline code overflowing on mobile. *Fix:* ~70ch measure, labelled share links, unclipped cover, `overflow-wrap` on inline code. *Command:* `$impeccable typeset`, `$impeccable adapt`.
- **[P2] Shared chrome is template-generic and inconsistent.** Rocket emoji logo, footer Twitter bird next to the hero's X, gray-200 focus rings, no active nav on `/blog`, a theme toggle that never changes icon. *Fix:* X icon, primary focus outline, Writing active on `/blog*`, sun/moon toggle with `aria-pressed`; the logo is the owner's call. *Command:* `$impeccable polish`.
- **[P3] Homepage repetition and line breaks.** Three featured projects repeat in the Open-source grid; the forced `<br />` strands "software," at 390 px. *Command:* `$impeccable layout`, `$impeccable polish`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Conference organizer:** three "Invite" labels, two destinations, the last a LinkedIn profile; 223/270/137 and "3 languages" vs "English or Hebrew" invite doubt; no email or logistics in the press kit; the loud gradient CTA feels salesy next to the editorial homepage.

**Developer arriving from a blog post:** very long lines, centered template header with unlabelled share icons, a pink upsell with a clipped cover at the end, no active nav showing the post belongs to Writing, sideways scrolling on phones from long inline code.

**Journalist wanting a bio:** "Bio & press kit" lives inside the speaking sub-site; the press kit itself is strong (short and full bio, download, at-a-glance), but has no contact email.
